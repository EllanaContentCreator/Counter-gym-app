# How Lana wants answers

- Keep answers SHORT. A few lines, not a wall of text.
- One step at a time. Don't list six steps when she only needs the next one.
- Plain words. No jargon. If a technical word is unavoidable, say what to tap, not what it means.
- Don't explain background, tradeoffs, or "two options" unless she asks.
- When she's following steps on her phone, give her the whole short list once so she isn't
  switching apps to re-read.
- Do the fiddly work for her wherever possible instead of handing her instructions.
